# Daily AGI Briefing — 2026-06-29

**Executive Summary:** Frontier AI deployment has entered a new phase where the US government, not just the labs, decides who gets access — OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and Anthropic's Mythos 5 are now both being released only to individually vetted partners, even as METR's own measurements show autonomous task length quietly doubling every ~105 days.

---

### 1. OpenAI Previews GPT-5.6 Sol Under Government-Vetted Access
**Source & Date:** OpenAI, The Washington Post, Axios — June 26, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Sol is OpenAI's most capable model yet, built for deeper reasoning, long-horizon agentic work, and technical domains including biology, genomics, and cybersecurity — but it's shipping to only ~20 government-approved partners rather than the public. Frontier capability and frontier deployment are now decoupled by policy, not just engineering, for the first time at this scale.
**Link:** https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/06/26/openai-says-us-government-will-vet-users-its-latest-ai-model/

### 2. US Government Clears Anthropic's Mythos 5 for 100+ Institutions
**Source & Date:** CNBC, TechCrunch, Bloomberg — June 26, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick authorized roughly 100 "trusted" companies and federal agencies to access Mythos 5 (Fable 5 remains blocked), citing defensive-cyber use cases. The episode is becoming a template: frontier model releases now run through case-by-case national-security review before broad rollout.
**Link:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html

### 3. METR Data Shows Autonomous Task Horizon Hit 16 Hours, Doubling Every ~105 Days
**Source & Date:** METR, AI Digest — data through March 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Claude Mythos Preview completed 16 hours of autonomous work in March 2026, up from GPT-4o's 4 minutes in mid-2024 — a 240x jump in under two years, with the doubling period accelerating from ~7 months to ~105 days. If the trend holds, METR's own benchmark suite is already too short to measure the next generation of models.
**Link:** https://theaidigest.org/time-horizons

### 4. Google Ships Gemini 3.5 Flash for Agentic and Coding Workloads
**Source & Date:** Google DeepMind — June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Gemini 3.5 Flash targets long-horizon agentic and coding tasks at Flash-tier speed and cost, outperforming Gemini 3.1 Pro on key benchmarks — but Google's flagships still rank outside the top five on several leaderboards, behind Anthropic, OpenAI, and Chinese labs. A capable-but-not-leading release from the lab with the most compute is itself a data point on how distributed frontier progress has become.
**Link:** https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/

### 5. Anthropic Accuses Alibaba of "Largest Known Distillation Attack" on Claude
**Source & Date:** CNBC — June 24, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Anthropic says Alibaba ran a systematic campaign to extract Claude's capabilities via distillation, calling it the largest such attack it has tracked. As frontier capability becomes the thing labs most need to protect, IP-extraction conflicts between US and Chinese labs are becoming a direct input into the safety and export-control debate, not just a competitive side issue.
**Link:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/anthropic-alibaba-distillation-campaign.html

### 6. Google DeepMind's Senior Research Exodus Continues
**Source & Date:** Fortune, Search Engine Journal — week of June 22–26, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Transformer co-inventor Noam Shazeer (to OpenAI) and AlphaFold lead/Nobel laureate John Jumper (to Anthropic), plus senior Gemini researchers Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel, have all left DeepMind for rivals. The architects of foundational pretraining and scientific-AI systems are concentrating at two labs, narrowing where the next architectural breakthroughs are likely to come from.
**Link:** https://fortune.com/2026/06/23/google-deepmind-ai-researcher-departures-raise-doubts-about-ability-to-win-the-ai-race-shazeer-jumper-eye-on-ai/

### 7. Anthropic Tracks Toward First Operating Profit on $47B Run-Rate
**Source & Date:** Industry reporting — June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Anthropic is approaching ~$559M in quarterly operating profit at a $47B annualized revenue run-rate, with Claude Code holding roughly 40% of the generative-AI coding market. Self-sustaining revenue at this scale removes a key practical constraint — funding — on continued frontier-scale training runs.
**Link:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/openai-anthropic-new-ai-spending-reality-as-users-shift-to-efficiency.html

### 8. Sakana AI's Fully Autonomous "AI Scientist" Pipeline Published in Nature
**Source & Date:** Sakana AI, Nature — June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** The AI Scientist system generates hypotheses, runs experiments, and writes up results without a human in the loop, and has now cleared peer review at a top-tier journal. Closing the loop on autonomous discovery — not just literature synthesis — remains one of the clearest practical tests of general intelligence.
**Link:** https://sakana.ai/ai-scientist-nature/

### 9. Coding Agents Still Plateau Within ~24 Hours While Top Humans Keep Improving
**Source & Date:** arXiv, "Scaling Test-time Compute for LLM Agents" — June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** In recent coding competitions, current agents plateau after roughly 24 hours of test-time compute while elite human competitors continue improving over much longer timeframes. This pinpoints long-horizon adaptation — not raw reasoning — as the capability still separating today's best agents from general intelligence.
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.12928

### 10. Government Oversight of Frontier AI Moves From Voluntary to Structural
**Source & Date:** International AI Safety Report 2026; NY RAISE Act; US executive order — ongoing through June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** The Bengio-led International AI Safety Report finds safeguard effectiveness still uncertain against sophisticated attackers even as safety-framework publication has doubled since 2025; meanwhile New York's RAISE Act and the federal pre-release review order now sit alongside the per-customer vetting seen in items #1–2. Oversight is consolidating into binding, case-by-case mechanisms rather than lab self-regulation.
**Link:** https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/
