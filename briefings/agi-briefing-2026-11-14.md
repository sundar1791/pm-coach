**Source:** The Neuron AI Digest — November 11, 2026  
**Link:** https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-wednesday-november-11-2026/

**Why it matters for AGI:** AI-assisted mathematical discovery — especially in formal domains like cryptography — is a leading indicator of AGI-adjacent reasoning. ### 6. Six Governments and Ten AI Companies Sign Paris AI Safety Declaration

**Source:** CASRAI — November 9, 2026  
**Link:** https://casrai.org/news/paris-call-ai-safety-security-joint-declaration-2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** International coordination on AI safety is essential for governing AGI-class systems. ### 7. OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Anthropic Back Shared Robots.txt Token

**Source:** The Neuron AI Digest — November 11, 2026  
**Link:** https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-wednesday-november-11-2026/

The four dominant AI companies reached a rare consensus: backing a shared token standard that lets publishers opt out of AI training via robots.txt. This is a voluntary industry-wide agreement on training data use.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Trust and legitimacy are prerequisites for deploying AGI-class systems at societal scale. A joint agreement on data consent represents the AI industry self-regulating in a domain where legislation has lagged — and sets a precedent for the kind of coordinated standards that AGI governance will require.

### 9. Brown University Publishes Ultrathin Bismuth Ferrite Film Breakthrough for AI Chips

**Source:** Nature Materials / ScienceDaily — November 12, 2026  
**Link:** https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/artificial_intelligence/

Brown University engineers demonstrated that bismuth ferrite films remain simultaneously magnetic and ferroelectric at thicknesses of a few angstroms — a materials science result with direct implications for ultra-low-power AI memory chips. Published in *Nature Materials* (DOI: 10.1038/s41563-026-02786-1).

**Why it matters for AGI:** AGI will require orders-of-magnitude improvements in compute efficiency to run at the scale needed for general reasoning. Ferroelectric materials in neuromorphic memory architectures could enable in-memory computing that dramatically reduces the energy cost of AI inference — a hardware prerequisite for always-on, embodied AGI systems.
