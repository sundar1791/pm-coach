### 1. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind Formally Coordinate on AI Safety

**Source:** Bloomberg / TechCrunch — September 15, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** This marks the first time the three leading frontier AI labs have publicly committed to joint safety coordination, signaling that the race to AGI is now being tempered by collective risk management. Dario Amodei's "Pace the Frontier" essay, backed by Sam Altman and Demis Hassabis, catalyzed the agreement — a rare moment of industry consensus that safety constraints may need to precede capability releases.
