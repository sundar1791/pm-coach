### 1. Google Shifts AI Leadership to California, Hassabis Becomes Chairman

**Source:** Bloomberg | August 6, 2026
**Link:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-06/google-shifts-ai-power-to-california-in-race-against-anthropic-openai

### 3. EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Obligations Now Enforceable

**Source:** TLT LLP AI Brief | August 2, 2026
**Link:** https://www.tlt.com/insights-and-events/insight/tlts-ai-brief-august-2026

The EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency requirements became legally enforceable on August 2, 2026, mandating clear labels on AI-generated content and disclosure when users interact with AI chatbots. Penalties up to 7% of global turnover apply for serious violations.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Regulatory infrastructure is hardening around AI systems just as they approach more general capabilities. This enforcement milestone sets a global precedent and will shape how AGI-adjacent systems are deployed commercially — potentially the biggest near-term constraint on agentic AI rollout.

### 4. AI Helps Disprove 87-Year-Old Jacobian Conjecture

**Source:** AI Research Breakthroughs — Skycrumbs Blog | July 2026
**Link:** https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026

Mathematician Levent Alpöge, working with Claude Fable 5, disproved the Jacobian Conjecture — a problem open since 1939 — through an explicit counterexample discovered by joint exploration of enormous proof spaces.

**Why it matters for AGI:** This is a landmark demonstration of AI contributing to frontier mathematical discovery, not just assisting with known methods. It signals that AI systems may be entering a phase where they can actively expand human knowledge in formal domains — a key capability marker on the AGI trajectory.

### 6. "Selective Activation Sparsity" — Smaller Models Match 3× Larger Models

**Source:** ICML 2026 / Skycrumbs Blog | July 2026
**Link:** https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026

Research presented at ICML 2026 introduced a training method called "selective activation sparsity," in which models learn to activate only the most relevant parameters per task. Models trained this way performed comparably to models three times their size on reasoning benchmarks.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Dramatic efficiency gains like this lower the compute cost of frontier-level reasoning, making AGI-capable systems accessible to more actors and accelerating the overall pace of development. Efficiency breakthroughs historically compress timelines.

### 7. AGI Benchmark Reality Check: Current AI Scores 4% on ARC-AGI-2

**Source:** TimeTrex / AI Insider | 2026
**Link:** https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/05/ai-trends-in-2026-what-is-artificial-general-intelligence-agi/

Despite widespread AGI claims, current AI systems score approximately 4% on the ARC-AGI-2 benchmark — designed to measure general abstract reasoning — while humans score near 100%. OpenAI's December 2024 system scored 87.5% on an earlier version of the test.

**Why it matters for AGI:** ARC-AGI-2 remains one of the most rigorous available proxies for general reasoning. The 4% score is a sobering signal that current transformer LLMs are far from general reasoning, even as their narrow capabilities continue to impress — reinforcing the gap between "functional AGI" (task automation) and "full AGI" (general reasoning).
