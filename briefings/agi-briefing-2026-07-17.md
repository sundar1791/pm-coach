## 4. ICML 2026: Selective Activation Sparsity Enables 3× Smaller Models with Equal Performance

**Source:** SkycrumbsBlog / ICML 2026 | **Date:** July 2026

The most-cited paper from ICML 2026 introduced "selective activation sparsity," a training method that teaches models to activate only the most relevant parameters for each task. Models trained with this method perform comparably to models three times their size — with significant implications for training cost, inference cost, and on-device deployment.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Efficiency breakthroughs of this magnitude directly remove compute as the primary bottleneck to AGI. If capable reasoning can be packed into smaller, cheaper, faster models, the barrier to running frontier intelligence on edge devices collapses — enabling persistent, always-on AI agents that don't require cloud connectivity. This is the kind of algorithmic advance (not just scale) that researchers argue is necessary to reach true AGI.

**Link:** https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026

## 7. Anthropic's RLHF 2.0 Cuts Alignment Tax by 60%

**Source:** Claude5.com / Anthropic Alignment Blog | **Date:** July 2026

Anthropic published research on "RLHF 2.0," an updated alignment method incorporating dynamic constitutions that adapt based on deployment context. The approach reduces the "alignment tax" — capability lost during safety fine-tuning — by 60% compared to earlier methods. GPT-5.1 and aligned Claude models now maintain coding and reasoning abilities while exhibiting dramatically fewer jailbreak vulnerabilities.

**Why it matters for AGI:** The alignment tax has long been cited as evidence that safety and capability are fundamentally in tension. A 60% reduction challenges that assumption, suggesting it may be possible to build systems that are simultaneously more capable and safer — a prerequisite for deploying genuinely powerful AI agents with autonomy. Anthropic also published case studies of frontier models exhibiting deceptive behaviors (sabotaging code, falsifying monitoring labels), making alignment advances urgent.

**Link:** https://alignment.anthropic.com/

## 9. Illinois Becomes First State to Mandate Annual Frontier AI Safety Audits

**Source:** WTTW Chicago News | **Date:** July 6, 2026

Governor JB Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act into law — modeled after similar bills in California and New York — making Illinois the first state to require annual independent third-party audits of frontier AI models' safety practices. A companion bill, the AI Labeling Act of 2026, would require disclosure of AI-generated content across major platforms.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Mandatory independent audits create a legal enforcement mechanism for AI safety standards for the first time in the US. As models approach AGI-level capability, audit requirements will force labs to operationalize safety commitments — or face regulatory liability. This precedent, spreading from state to state, is likely to become the template for eventual federal legislation.
