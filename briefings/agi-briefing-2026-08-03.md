### 1. EU AI Act Enforcement Begins — August 2, 2026

**Source & Date:** European Commission, August 2, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** The EU AI Act's core enforcement phase is now active, requiring all AI systems interacting with users (chatbots, deepfakes, AI-generated content) to be transparently labelled. The European AI Office gains full penalty powers over general-purpose AI model providers — including GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Llama — for the first time. This is the world's first comprehensive legal framework actively governing frontier AI, and it sets the template for global AI governance as systems approach AGI-level capabilities.

**Link:** [Commission starts enforcing AI Act rules — European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714)

### 2. Claude Fable 5 Helps Disprove 87-Year-Old Jacobian Conjecture

**Source & Date:** The Next Web / CoinDesk / multiple, July 19–21, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Mathematician Levent Alpöge, working with Claude Fable 5, produced a 216-character polynomial counterexample disproving the Jacobian Conjecture (open since 1939, listed among Smale's 18 problems for the 21st century). The result has been independently verified and formalized in Lean. This is the most significant AI-assisted mathematical breakthrough to date — a concrete demonstration that frontier AI can help humans solve problems beyond the reach of prior generations of researchers, which is a key capability marker on the path to AGI.

**Link:** [AI just disproved the 87-year-old Jacobian conjecture — The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/jacobian-conjecture-disproved-ai-fable-5)
