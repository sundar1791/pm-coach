## 1. Anthropic Surpasses OpenAI in Revenue Trajectory

**Source:** Fortune | July 2, 2026
**Link:** https://fortune.com/2026/07/02/sam-altman-new-world-order-ai-openai-google-anthropic/

**Why it matters for AGI:** Anthropic has reported being on course to hit $47 billion in revenue and expects to be profitable by 2029 — a year ahead of OpenAI. ## 8. Illinois Signs Landmark AI Safety Measures Act

**Source:** WTTW Chicago News | July 6, 2026
**Link:** https://news.wttw.com/2026/07/06/pritzker-signs-landmark-ai-regulation-bill-aims-mitigate-risks

**Why it matters for AGI:** Governor Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act establishing new reporting standards for AI models capable of facilitating large-scale harms — including CBRN weapon assistance and cyberattacks. It may become a template for federal legislation.
