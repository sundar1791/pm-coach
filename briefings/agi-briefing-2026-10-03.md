## 10. First Complete Adult Brain Map Built; AI Plays Central Role

**Source:** ScienceDaily / Multiple — October 2026  
**Why it matters for AGI:** Scientists from Google, HHMI Janelia, and Cambridge University completed the first comprehensive neural map of an adult male fruit fly — 166,000 neurons and 12,000 cell types assembled via AI-driven image stitching into a navigable 3D model. Understanding biological neural architectures at this resolution provides foundational blueprints for neuromorphic and biologically-inspired AGI systems, and demonstrates AI's ability to accelerate scientific discovery at scale.
