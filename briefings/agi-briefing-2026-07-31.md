### 1. AI Disproves 87-Year-Old Jacobian Conjecture

**Source:** AI by AI Weekly / Champaign Magazine | **Date:** July 20–26, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Mathematician Levent Alpöge disproved the Jacobian Conjecture — unsolved since 1939 — using Claude Fable 5 to discover an explicit counterexample. The result was immediately formalized in Lean and submitted to Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures repo. This is a landmark demonstration that AI can operate as a genuine creative collaborator in open-ended scientific discovery, not just pattern matching or calculation — a key capability threshold on the path to AGI.

**Link:** https://champaignmagazine.com/2026/07/26/ai-by-ai-weekly-top-5-july-20-26-2026/
