### 2. AI Agents Hit 66% on OSWorld — 6 Points Shy of Human Baseline

**Source & Date:** Bloomberg / InvestorPlace — September 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** OSWorld measures whether AI agents can complete real tasks across computer operating systems — a direct proxy for general-purpose autonomous action. Going from ~12% to 66% in a single year (with human baseline at 72%) shows an extraordinary rate of convergence. This kind of generalized computer-use capability is one of the core requirements for practical AGI.

**Link:** [InvestorPlace — AI's Biggest Breakthrough Is 80% Here](https://investorplace.com/smartmoney/2026/09/ais-biggest-breakthrough-80-here-how-to-play/)

### 3. Nvidia's Nemotron Coding Model Beats Top Human at IOI 2026

**Source & Date:** MEAN CEO Blog / AI Weekly — September 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Nvidia's Nemotron-3-Ultra-CC scored 535.4/600 at the International Olympiad in Informatics, surpassing the top human contestant's 498.27. Competitive programming at the olympiad level represents some of the most complex, creative reasoning humans perform. While the result is from Nvidia's own preprint and awaits independent replication, it represents a significant data point in superhuman coding ability.
