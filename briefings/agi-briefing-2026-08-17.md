### 3. OSWorld Benchmark: AI Jumps from 12% to 66% in One Year

**Source:** 2026 Stanford AI Index — August 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** OSWorld tests autonomous computer use — navigating GUIs, completing multi-step desktop tasks — an ability closely tied to real-world agent deployment. This rate of improvement, if sustained, means parity within months.

### 4. DeepMind Paper: "Reasoning Collapse" Identified and Partially Solved

**Source:** AI News Week of August 3–9, 2026 — Medium / David Akpovi

**Why it matters for AGI:** DeepMind published research identifying "reasoning collapse" — a systematic failure mode where models fail on logically equivalent problems stated differently. Their proposed training methodology reduces brittleness by ~40% on their benchmark. Understanding and patching structural reasoning failures is critical for AGI reliability; an AGI that reasons inconsistently across surface-level rephrasing cannot be trusted for autonomous high-stakes tasks.

**Link:** https://medium.com/@davidakpovi/ai-news-week-of-august-3-9-2026-8dfa677ffca3
