## 1. OpenAI AI Disproves the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH)

**Source:** Gigazine / unite.ai / The Neuron — October 27, 2026
**Links:** https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20261027-openai-three-new-discoveries | https://www.unite.ai/openai-says-its-models-produced-three-proofs-in-math-cs-and-physics/

**Why it matters for AGI:** The ETH is a longstanding conjecture in computational complexity theory that has resisted proof for decades. OpenAI's models not only produced the disproof but formalized it in Lean, the proof assistant — making it machine-verifiable. If confirmed by the broader community, this would be the first time AI generated a result that fundamentally changes how computer scientists think about computational limits. It represents AI doing novel science, not reproducing known results.

## 2. OpenAI AI Produces Three Research Proofs in Math, CS, and Physics

**Source:** OpenAI / The Neuron — October 27, 2026
**Links:** https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/ | https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-tuesday-october-27-2026/

**Why it matters for AGI:** Beyond the ETH disproof, OpenAI's models produced a result on the Kakeya conjecture in harmonic analysis and formalized Yang–Mills existence (a Millennium Prize problem in quantum field theory) in Lean.
