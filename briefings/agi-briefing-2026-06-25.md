# Daily AGI Briefing — 2026-06-25

**Executive Summary:** Google's AI-talent exodus deepened today — with Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel joining Shazeer and Jumper in leaving DeepMind for Anthropic and OpenAI, erasing $270B from Alphabet's market cap — while GPT-5.6's launch window slipped to July, leaving Anthropic's newly-shipped Claude Fable 5/Mythos 5 as the most recent frontier-model move.

### 1. Google DeepMind's Talent Exodus Widens — $270B Wiped from Alphabet
**Source & Date:** Bloomberg / TechCrunch / Taipei Times, June 22-24, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Beyond Tuesday's departures of Transformer co-inventor Noam Shazeer (to OpenAI) and Nobel laureate John Jumper (to Anthropic), two more senior Gemini researchers — Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel — are also leaving DeepMind for Anthropic. A four-person brain drain from a single lab to its two closest rivals, in one week, materially shifts where frontier reasoning research talent — and likely near-term capability gains — will concentrate.
**Link:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/google-poised-to-lose-two-more-high-profile-ai-staffers-to-anthropic

### 2. GPT-5.6 Launch Slips to July as Prediction Markets Reverse
**Source & Date:** Industry coverage, June 24, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Odds of a GPT-5.6 release in the June 22-28 window collapsed from ~83% to 18% within days. Slipping iteration cycles on flagship reasoning models — after a string of rapid releases earlier in 2026 — is a useful signal for whether frontier capability gains are still compounding quickly or beginning to hit diminishing returns/safety-review friction.
**Link:** https://andrew.ooo/answers/what-is-gpt-5-6-release-june-2026/

### 3. Anthropic Ships Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5
**Source & Date:** Anthropic / Claude API Docs, June 9, 2026 (still the most recent frontier release as of today)
**Why it matters for AGI:** Fable 5 is Anthropic's most capable widely-released model, with state-of-the-art results on nearly all benchmarks, always-on adaptive thinking, a 1M-token context window, and automatic safety-classifier fallback to Opus 4.8 for flagged cyber/bio requests. Pairing frontier capability with built-in misuse-routing safeguards is a concrete template for scaling general capability without scaling unmitigated risk in lockstep.
**Link:** https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5

### 4. AI-Designed Vaccine Completes Human Trials
**Source & Date:** University of Cambridge, reported this week (June 2026)
**Why it matters for AGI:** This marks the first time a vaccine's key component was designed entirely by AI and successfully trialed in humans. A general intelligence should be able to make real scientific contributions outside narrow benchmarks — AI-originated biomedical design reaching human trials is concrete evidence of that kind of generalization, not just text/code fluency.
**Link:** https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates

### 5. Photonic Computing Breakthrough Could Slash AI Energy Costs 246x
**Source & Date:** University of Pennsylvania research, reported this week
**Why it matters for AGI:** Researchers built a hybrid light-matter computing system that processes diagnostic-grade image analysis 246x more efficiently than conventional GPUs (0.8ms vs 85ms per scan) at expert-level accuracy. Compute and energy cost are widely seen as a hard ceiling on how far current architectures can scale toward more general capability — order-of-magnitude efficiency gains directly loosen that ceiling.
**Link:** https://www.devflokers.com/blog/ai-news-may-2026-models-papers-open-source

### 6. Study: Top LLMs Still Fail Classic Psychological Attention Tests at Scale
**Source & Date:** Research coverage, June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Leading models handle short attention-test lists correctly but degrade sharply as task length and complexity grow — a pattern mirrored in other recent evaluations of long-context reasoning. This is a recurring reality check: scalable failures on simple cognitive-psychology paradigms suggest a persistent gap between benchmark performance and the kind of robust, length-invariant reasoning associated with general intelligence.
**Link:** https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates

### 7. UC San Diego Scholars Argue Current LLMs Already Meet a Reasonable AGI Bar
**Source & Date:** UC San Diego Today, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Four UCSD scholars concluded that, by reasonable functional standards, today's frontier LLMs already constitute a form of AGI — while acknowledging these systems still can't autonomously learn genuinely new tasks or expand their own problem-solving scope. The debate underscores that "AGI" lacks an agreed operational definition, which matters for how labs, governments, and the public calibrate readiness and risk.
**Link:** https://today.ucsd.edu/story/is-artificial-general-intelligence-here

### 8. Trump Executive Order's Voluntary Pre-Release Review Regime Continues to Take Shape
**Source & Date:** NPR, June 2, 2026 (ongoing implementation)
**Why it matters for AGI:** The order asks AI companies to voluntarily submit their most powerful models for government testing up to 30 days before public release and directs agencies to build cyber-capability benchmarks for frontier systems. As frontier labs ship faster (Fable 5, Mythos 5) while next releases slip (GPT-5.6), how this review regime is actually exercised will shape the cadence at which AGI-track capability reaches the public.
**Link:** https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order

### 9. International AI Safety Report: Safety Frameworks Doubling, Gaps Persist
**Source & Date:** International AI Safety Report, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Since 2025, the number of companies publishing frontier AI safety frameworks has more than doubled, and detection/training techniques for safer models have improved — but sophisticated attackers can still bypass current defenses, and real-world safeguard effectiveness remains uncertain. This tension between rising safety commitments and unresolved robustness gaps is central to whether increasingly general systems can be deployed safely.
**Link:** https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/
