### 7. AI Backdoor Survives Post-Training: 0.1% Corpus Poisoning Sufficient

**Source:** Anthropic Fellows, Anthropic, UC Berkeley & UIUC Research | November 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** New research shows that poisoning just 0.1% of a pretraining corpus can embed a remote code execution backdoor that survives the entire post-training pipeline into a deployed CLI agent. As AI systems gain more agentic autonomy — a key step toward AGI — this class of supply-chain attack becomes existentially dangerous. The finding underscores that training data integrity is a critical safety frontier that must be solved before AGI systems can be trusted.

**Link:** [AI Papers of the Week](https://academy.dair.ai/papers)
