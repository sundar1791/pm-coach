## 5. DeepMind Paper: Training Method Reduces "Reasoning Collapse" by 40%

**Source:** Skycrumbs Blog / AI Research August 2026 | **Date:** August 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** DeepMind identified a critical failure mode called "reasoning collapse," in which frontier models lose coherent reasoning when problems are reformulated in novel ways. Reducing distributional fragility is a prerequisite for deploying AGI-grade systems reliably across diverse real-world tasks.
