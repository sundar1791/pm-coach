## 2. OpenAI DevDay 2026: "Dots" Persistent Agents & Computer Use in Agents API

**Source & Date:** OpenAI Developer Community — October 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** "Dots" are AI agents that continue making progress on tasks *between* conversations — a direct step toward continuous, autonomous operation rather than single-session tools. Computer use in the Agents API lets AI directly operate software UIs, extending autonomous action to virtually any digital system. These two capabilities together represent a shift from reactive AI to proactive, persistent agents.

## 3. OpenAI, Anthropic & Google in Active Safety Collaboration — Standards Body Forming

**Source & Date:** Bloomberg / TechCrunch / CNBC — September 15, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** The three dominant frontier-AI labs are coordinating on safety for the first time, including embedding third-party evaluators inside each company to monitor for dangerous capabilities. This is the most significant industry-level safety governance development of the year.

## 8. Grok 4.20 Introduces Parallel Reasoning Architecture (4-Agent Synthesis)

**Source & Date:** xAI — February 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Grok 4.20's architecture routes queries to four specialized reasoning agents that think in parallel, then synthesizes outputs into a single coherent response. This multi-agent-within-a-model approach mirrors cognitive modularity theories of human intelligence and represents a departure from monolithic transformer inference. It may be a harbinger of hybrid architectures that dominate the next generation of models.

**Link:** https://www.shakudo.io/blog/top-9-large-language-models

## 9. ARC-AGI-2 Benchmark Reality Check: AI Scores ~4%, Humans ~100%

**Source & Date:** Medium / ARC Prize — 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Despite industry marketing about imminent AGI, the updated ARC-AGI-2 benchmark — designed to measure core fluid intelligence — shows AI at roughly 4% versus human performance near 100%. This is the most important calibration tool for separating hype from actual progress. It suggests current models, despite impressive domain performance, lack the generalized novel-task reasoning that characterizes human intelligence.

**Link:** https://medium.com/@svnkrmkr/what-is-agi-the-2026-reality-check-on-artificial-general-intelligence-25b30223a7b3
