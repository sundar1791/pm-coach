### 4. ICML 2026: "Selective Activation Sparsity" Enables 3× More Efficient Models

**Source:** Skycrumbs AI Research Blog / AI Research Breakthroughs July 2026  
**Why it matters for AGI:** New research presented at ICML 2026 introduces "selective activation sparsity" — a training method that teaches models to activate only the most relevant parameters per task. Models trained this way perform comparably to models three times their size on reasoning benchmarks. This dramatically lowers the compute cost of frontier reasoning, making AGI-capable systems more accessible and deployable.
