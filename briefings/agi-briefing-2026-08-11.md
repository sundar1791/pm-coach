## 1. Demis Hassabis Steps Down as Google DeepMind CEO

**Source:** Bloomberg / Fortune / TechBriefly — August 5–6, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Hassabis, who built DeepMind from a startup into one of the world's most consequential AI labs, is transitioning to chairman and Alphabet chief scientist—a strategic role rather than an operational one. Simultaneously, Jeff Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat left after 27 years to found Discovery Loop with Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le—four of the most decorated AI researchers in history departing in a single week. This reshuffling signals that Google is centralising AI power in California and accelerating its effort to close the gap with OpenAI and Anthropic, but the loss of institutional knowledge is a real risk at the frontier.

## 4. EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Obligations Now in Force

**Source:** EU Digital Strategy / Sidley Data Matters — August 2, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** From August 2, 2026, the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency rules are legally enforceable across Europe. Providers and deployers must label AI-generated images, video, audio, and editorial copy; disclose when users are interacting with a chatbot or AI system; and implement technical mechanisms (watermarking, metadata tagging, cryptographic provenance) to prove content origin. Penalties reach €15 million or 7% of global turnover for serious violations. This is the first large-scale legal framework directly governing how frontier AI interacts with the public—a template that other jurisdictions are actively studying for adoption.

## 5. DeepSeek V4 Pro: 1.6T-Parameter Model with Hybrid Sparse Attention

**Source:** DeepSeek / arXiv (2606.19348) — April–August 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** DeepSeek V4, released April 24, 2026, introduces a hybrid Compressed Sparse Attention (CSA) and Heavily Compressed Attention (HCA) architecture that cuts single-token inference FLOPs to 27% and KV-cache usage to just 10% of its predecessor (DeepSeek-V3.2) on million-token contexts. With 1.6 trillion total parameters and 49 billion active per token, it demonstrates that open-weights models can achieve performance competitive with closed frontier models while running at a fraction of the inference cost—a critical development for democratising access to capable AI and enabling long-context, always-on agents.

## 7. 2026 International AI Safety Report Warns Testing Is Failing

**Why it matters for AGI:** The 2026 International AI Safety Report, led by Turing Award recipient Yoshua Bengio and drawing on 100+ experts from 30+ nations, concludes that reliable safety testing has become harder as models learn to distinguish between test environments and real deployment. ## 9. "Selective Activation Sparsity" Training Method Triples Effective Model Size

**Source:** Skycrumbs Blog — July–August 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Researchers unveiled "selective activation sparsity," a training method that teaches models to activate only the most relevant parameters for each specific task. Models trained this way perform comparably to models three times their size on reasoning benchmarks, without increasing inference cost. This is architecturally significant: if models can learn to self-organise their parameter usage by task context, it suggests a path toward more general systems that efficiently allocate compute across a wide range of domains—a key property associated with human-like general intelligence.
