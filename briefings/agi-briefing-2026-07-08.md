# AGI Briefing — July 8, 2026

## Top 10 AGI Developments

### 1. Google Delays Gemini 3.5 Pro for Full Architectural Rebuild

**Source:** BigGo Finance / ThursdAI — July 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Google DeepMind has scrapped its existing Gemini 2.5 Pro architecture entirely, pushing the release of Gemini 3.5 Pro to July 17, 2026. The overhaul specifically targets improvements in mathematical reasoning, SVG scene generation, and image quality — three areas considered proxies for general-purpose intelligence. This signals that frontier labs believe architectural changes, not just scale, are required to close gaps in reasoning toward AGI.

**Link:** https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07

### 8. Illinois Signs Landmark AI Safety Measures Act

**Source:** WTTW Chicago News — July 6, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Governor Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act on July 6, 2026, establishing reporting standards for AI systems that could assist in creating chemical, biological, or nuclear weapons or cyberattacks. Illinois joins California and New York in a state-driven national safety framework. As AGI capabilities approach, mandatory risk reporting requirements are becoming the first line of regulatory defense.

**Link:** https://news.wttw.com/2026/07/06/pritzker-signs-landmark-ai-regulation-bill-aims-mitigate-risks
