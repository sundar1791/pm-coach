## Top 10 AGI Developments

### 1. Anthropic Recruits AlphaFold Nobel Laureate & Launches Claude for Science
**Source:** TechTimes / FourWeekMBA / DigitalApplied — June–July 2026

John Jumper, co-creator of AlphaFold and 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry co-recipient, joined Anthropic on June 19, 2026 after nearly nine years at Google DeepMind. On June 30, Anthropic held "The Briefing: AI for Science" in San Francisco, showcasing Claude Mythos 5, which achieved approximately 10× faster drug-design workflows and autonomously hit 9 of 14 protein targets without human intervention. This represents a landmark fusion of frontier LLMs with Nobel-caliber scientific expertise, and the autonomous protein targeting demonstrates task-specific superintelligence in biology — one of AGI's historically hardest challenges.

### 2. OpenAI & Broadcom Unveil Jalapeño: First Custom LLM Inference Chip
**Source:** TechCrunch / VentureBeat / Tom's Hardware — June 24, 2026

OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño, OpenAI's first custom AI accelerator — a reticle-sized ASIC purpose-built for LLM inference, developed in just nine months (potentially the fastest high-performance ASIC development cycle on record). Early tests show substantially better performance-per-watt than current NVIDIA GPUs, with target deployment cost estimated 50% below Nvidia equivalents. OpenAI used its own AI models to accelerate parts of the chip design process, demonstrating a self-referential AI-accelerating-AI loop that will drive rapid infrastructure scaling. Deployment targets late 2026, with full-scale production ramp in H1 2028.

### 3. Anthropic Publishes Modular Dual-Use Knowledge Isolation Technique
**Source:** Anthropic Alignment Blog / Claude5 Hub — July 2026

Anthropic published research on a method for isolating dual-use knowledge to specific modules within a language model, where these modules can be switched on or off to control what the model knows about dangerous topics. This is a significant alignment breakthrough because it allows fine-grained capability control without degrading overall model performance — a key requirement for safely deploying AGI-level systems.

### 5. Illinois Signs Landmark AI Safety Measures Act
**Source:** WTTW Chicago News — July 6, 2026

Governor JB Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act on July 6, 2026, establishing new mandatory reporting standards for AI models that could assist in creating chemical, biological, or nuclear weapons or enable large-scale cyberattacks. Illinois joins California and New York in establishing statutory AI safety requirements. This represents an accelerating US state-level regulatory framework that will shape how AGI-capable systems can legally be deployed domestically.

### 9. Transformer Layer Training Breakthrough Slashes RL Fine-Tuning Cost
**Source:** IntoAI Research Weekly — July 1–8, 2026

Researchers published findings showing that training a single transformer layer can recover most of the performance gains achieved by full-parameter reinforcement learning training — and in some cases surpass them. High-contribution layers are typically found in the middle of the transformer stack. This efficiency result could dramatically reduce the compute cost of aligning and capability-tuning large models, lowering the barrier to AGI-level performance for organizations with constrained resources.
