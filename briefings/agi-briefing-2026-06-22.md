# Daily AGI Briefing — June 22, 2026

**Executive summary:** The most concrete AGI-relevant signal today is capability, not policy: an upgraded Gemini 3 Deep Think reportedly solved 18 previously unsolved research problems in math, physics, and computer science — including a decade-old open conjecture — while in parallel the US government's national-security export ban on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 (triggered by a reported jailbreak) remains unresolved nine days in, underscoring how governance is struggling to keep pace with frontier capability.

## 1. Gemini 3 Deep Think Solves 18 Previously Unsolved Research Problems
**Source & Date:** Google DeepMind blog / VentureBeat / Winbuzzer, ongoing through June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** The upgraded reasoning mode, built on Gemini 3.1 Pro, reportedly solved 18 open problems across mathematics, physics, and computer science — including a submodular-optimization conjecture mathematicians had been unable to prove since 2015 — plus gold-medal-level results on the 2025 International Physics and Chemistry Olympiads. Autonomously cracking problems that resisted human experts for a decade is one of the clearest demonstrations yet of general (not narrowly trained) scientific reasoning.
**Link:** https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-deep-think/

## 2. US Export Ban on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Remains Unresolved
**Source & Date:** Anthropic / Fortune / National Law Review, directive issued June 12, 2026, ongoing
**Why it matters for AGI:** The Commerce Department invoked national-security export-control authority to bar Anthropic from serving its most capable models, Fable 5 and Mythos 5, to any foreign national worldwide — reportedly triggered by Amazon discovering a jailbreak in Mythos 5 — forcing Anthropic to take both models offline globally. Anthropic argues recalling a deployed frontier model over a single narrow jailbreak would, if applied industry-wide, halt all new model releases; nine days on with no resolution, this is the most direct case yet of a government physically severing public access to a frontier-capability model on safety grounds.
**Link:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access

## 10. New York's RAISE Act Frontier-Model Safety Law Moves Toward 2027 Enforcement
**Source & Date:** Governor Hochul's office / IAPP / Wiley, signed into law, takes effect January 1, 2027
**Why it matters for AGI:** The RAISE Act requires large frontier developers (>$500M revenue) to publish safety protocols and report incidents within 72 hours, backed by an oversight office and civil penalties up to $3M per violation — closely tracking California's parallel Transparency in Frontier AI Act, with both OpenAI and Anthropic publicly endorsing it. Two of the largest state economies converging on near-identical frontier-model disclosure rules is the closest thing to a binding US regulatory floor for AGI-relevant systems so far, even as the International AI Safety Report concludes sophisticated attackers can still bypass most current safeguards.
**Link:** https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-signs-nation-leading-legislation-require-ai-frameworks-ai-frontier-models
