### 1. OpenAI Announces New Model Development Pacing & Alignment Safeguards
**Source:** AIGC News / OpenAI — *August 18, 2026*

### 2. Google's AI Leadership Overhaul: Demis Hassabis Steps Back, Koray Kavukcuoglu Takes Over
**Source:** Bloomberg / CNBC — *August 6–12, 2026*

Google is consolidating its AI leadership in Mountain View, CA. Demis Hassabis, DeepMind's co-founder, has stepped back from day-to-day operations to become Chairman of Google DeepMind and Alphabet's Chief Scientist. Koray Kavukcuoglu now runs Google's sprawling AI research and operations.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Structural changes at the lab that created AlphaFold and Gemini signal Google is reorganising for a faster-paced AGI race. Centralising leadership under a single operational head may accelerate decision-making and model deployment timelines.

**Link:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/google-deepmind-koray-kavukcuoglu.html

### 3. Jeff Dean Leaves Google to Found AI-for-Science Startup Discovery Loop
**Source:** AI Watch / Bloomberg — *August 6, 2026*

Jeff Dean, who joined Google in 1999 and helped build its AI infrastructure, is departing to found Discovery Loop — a startup aimed at using AI to accelerate scientific discovery across domains.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Dean's departure from Google signals that top talent sees the next frontier as AI-driven science, not just better chatbots. A dedicated AGI-adjacent research company focused on scientific discovery represents a new attack vector on the hardest open problems.

### 4. DeepMind Publishes "Reasoning Collapse" Research — Proposes Fix That Cuts Brittleness 40%
**Source:** AI Research Highlights / Skycrumbs — *August 2026*

DeepMind published research identifying "reasoning collapse" in current reasoning models — where models catastrophically fail on logically equivalent problems phrased differently. A new training methodology reduced this brittleness by ~40% on their benchmark.

**Why it matters for AGI:** Robust, generalizable reasoning is a core AGI prerequisite. If reasoning models fail on surface-level problem reformulations, they cannot be trusted for novel real-world tasks. A 40% reduction in collapse is meaningful progress toward more reliable general intelligence.

**Link:** https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026

### 10. EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Obligations Now Legally Enforceable
**Source:** AI Safety / Alignment coverage — *August 2, 2026*

The EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations became legally enforceable on August 2, 2026. Companies must now clearly label AI-generated or AI-modified content and disclose when users are interacting with AI systems.

**Why it matters for AGI:** As AI systems approach and potentially reach AGI, regulatory frameworks governing disclosure and accountability become critical guardrails. The EU's move is the first major enforcement milestone of comprehensive AI law, likely to shape global norms.
