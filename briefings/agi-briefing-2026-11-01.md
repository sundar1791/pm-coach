## 1. OpenAI GPT-6 Astra Hits 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3

**Source:** Bloomberg / TechXplore, September 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** ARC-AGI-3 is the most rigorous general-intelligence benchmark available; a near-perfect score marks a qualitative leap past every prior model. OpenAI itself said Astra "might one day be seen as the early stages of artificial general intelligence." TechXplore notes the result doesn't settle the AGI question but makes it harder than ever to answer definitively. The benchmark was explicitly designed to be resistant to pattern memorization, so this signals genuine reasoning capability.

## 2. OpenAI's Unreleased Model Proves Results in Math, CS, and Physics

**Source:** AI Weekly / AI Daily Digest, late October 2026

## 3. Axiom AI Completes Machine-Checked Proof of Classification of Finite Simple Groups

**Source:** AI Daily Digest / How Machines Think, late October 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** The Classification of Finite Simple Groups is one of the most complex mathematical proofs in history — running to tens of thousands of pages across hundreds of papers authored over decades. Axiom's automated agent swarm completing the first fully machine-checked version is a landmark for mathematical AI and demonstrates that multi-agent AI systems can tackle challenges far beyond any single human's capacity to verify.
**Link:** https://howmachinesthink.substack.com/p/ai-update-october-week-5

## 9. Interpretability Breakthrough: Linear Probe Catches Rule-Breaking Before Output

**Source:** AI Daily Digest (November 1, 2026), arxiv
**Why it matters for AGI:** A new interpretability paper demonstrates a linear probe — a simple learned classifier applied to a model's internal states — that can detect when an agent is about to violate a rule before it generates any output. Every deployed AI guardrail today reads the output after the fact; this shifts safety intervention to intent-detection. For AGI alignment, early-warning systems at the representation level are critical for controlling autonomous agents.
**Link:** https://github.com/diclogic/ai-daily-digest/issues/200
