## 10. Reasoning-Model Paradigm ("Slow Thinking") Continues to Mature Across Labs
**Source & Date:** Research roundup (arXiv survey literature), ongoing through June 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Following OpenAI's o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1, and Gemini's reasoning models, the field continues to converge on deliberate, multi-step "slow thinking" (chain-of-thought, tree-of-thought, self-consistency, RL-tuned reasoning) as the dominant path toward more general problem-solving, rather than purely scaling fast, heuristic next-token prediction. The continued convergence across labs on this paradigm is one of the better leading indicators of where near-term AGI-relevant capability gains will come from.
