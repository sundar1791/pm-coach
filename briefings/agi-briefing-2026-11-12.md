# Daily AGI Briefing — 2026-11-12

**Executive Summary:** Harvard, Anthropic, and QuEra published a preprint demonstrating AI-designed quantum circuits that reduce resource requirements by up to 39x, potentially accelerating the quantum-classical hybrid computing capabilities that underpin long-horizon AGI research.

## Top 10 AGI-Relevant Developments

### 1. AI-Designed Quantum Circuits Cut Resource Requirements by 39x
**Source:** PR Newswire / Harvard, Anthropic, QuEra | **Date:** November 10, 2026

A joint preprint from Harvard, Anthropic, and QuEra demonstrates AI-optimized quantum circuits that dramatically reduce Toffoli gate counts — from ~24,000 down to 1,583 in one case — and achieve combined resource reductions of up to 39x. This matters for AGI because quantum-enhanced computation could unlock problem domains (protein folding, cryptography, optimization) currently intractable for classical AI, and the ability to design better quantum circuits autonomously is itself a marker of advanced AI reasoning.

**Link:** https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/harvard-anthropic-and-quera-breakthrough-research-uniquely-shows-ai-can-reduce-the-resources-quantum-applications-need-significantly-accelerating-the-era-of-useful-quantum-computing-302934155.html

### 2. MIT CSAIL: AI Can Read and Rewrite Neural Network Weights
**Source:** HackerNoon / MIT CSAIL | **Date:** November 10, 2026

MIT CSAIL published research showing a frontier model can edit a smaller model's weights without gradient descent — essentially "programming" a neural network's internal representations directly. This is AGI-relevant because weight-space editing would allow AI systems to modify their own or other models' knowledge and capabilities in targeted ways, a capability associated with recursive self-improvement.

**Link:** https://hackernoon.com/ai-can-now-read-and-rewrite-neural-network-weights-heres-why-it-matters

### 7. Google DeepMind AI Agents Probe 1024-bit RSA Cryptographic Weaknesses
**Source:** AI News Nov 8 / AIdapted | **Date:** November 6, 2026

Google DeepMind researchers used AI agents to help identify two structural weaknesses in 1024-bit RSA cryptographic systems. While the RSA-1024 standard is already considered legacy, using AI as an autonomous mathematical-reasoning agent to find cryptographic flaws demonstrates advancing capability in formal reasoning and adversarial problem-solving — core AGI competencies.

**Link:** https://aidapted.ro/en/articles/ai-news-8-november-2026/

### 8. Paris Call Joint Declaration on AI Safety Signed by Major Labs
**Source:** Government of Canada / PM Statement | **Date:** November 9, 2026

Heads of state and major AI labs including Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Mistral AI signed the Paris Call joint declaration on AI safety and security, committing to strict compliance with safety standards and independent third-party evaluations. The declaration also flags risks from safeguards being stripped from open-weight models. This governance milestone matters for AGI because international coordination on safety standards directly shapes how powerful systems are developed and deployed.

**Link:** https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/statements/2026/11/09/joint-declaration-paris-call-ai-safety-and-security

### 9. New York Launches AI Safety Research Center (NYCAIRR)
**Source:** NY Governor's Office | **Date:** ~November 2026

Governor Hochul launched the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research (NYCAIRR) at Binghamton University, focused on independent testing of frontier AI systems, foundational research, and training AI safety experts. Simultaneously, New York's RAISE Act portal opened for frontier AI companies to register safety and reporting requirements. Institutional capacity for independent AI evaluation is essential infrastructure for safe AGI development.

**Link:** https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-declares-ny-will-lead-nation-ai-science-and-safety-launches-new-research

### 10. Australia Announces New AI Safety Regulator for Frontier Developers
**Source:** The Conversation | **Date:** ~November 2026

Australia announced a new AI safety regulator targeting frontier AI developers, with mandatory reporting requirements for misaligned behavior and cyber incidents, and potential criminal law changes for serious misconduct. The global spread of binding regulatory frameworks for frontier AI — alongside the Paris Call declaration — signals that governments are treating AGI-class systems as a near-term risk requiring structural oversight.
