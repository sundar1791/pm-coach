### 2. UN Bridgetown Special Summit on AI Safety Opens Today

### 5. Paper: "Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights"
**Source & Date:** arXiv (mid-November 2026), via AI/ML Research Digest

### 6. SAFE AI Act Outline Released by US Congress
**Source & Date:** CASRAI / US Representatives Beyer, Jacobs & Maloy, November 19, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** The two-page SAFE AI Act outline would require frontier AI labs to undergo safety evaluations before deploying new models, while exempting academics. This is the most substantive US federal AI safety legislation to emerge in 2026, potentially establishing binding pre-deployment review requirements that could shape how AGI development proceeds.
**Link:** https://casrai.org/news/safe-ai-act-outline-frontier-ai-labs-2026
