### 1. OpenAI Reveals — Then Suspends — "Astra" Flagship Model Family

### 2. Google DeepMind Undergoes Major Leadership Restructuring

**Source & Date:** Bloomberg / CNBC — August 5–12, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Demis Hassabis, DeepMind's co-founder and CEO, has stepped back from day-to-day leadership to become Chairman and Alphabet's Chief Scientist, with Koray Kavukcuoglu now running AI research and operations. Simultaneously, Jeff Dean — a 27-year Google veteran and foundational figure in modern deep learning — departed to start a new company called "Discovery Loop." This is the most significant leadership change in the AGI race, potentially reshaping research priorities and competitive dynamics across the entire field.

**Link:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/google-deepmind-koray-kavukcuoglu.html

### 6. Gemini 3.1 Pro Achieves 77.1% on ARC-AGI-2 Benchmark

**Source & Date:** TechTarget / LLM Stats — February 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Gemini 3.1 Pro scored 77.1% on the ARC-AGI-2 benchmark — a test specifically designed to measure abstract reasoning closer to human general intelligence — while also featuring a 1-million-token context window with multimodal reasoning across text, images, audio, video, and code. ### 7. DeepMind Publishes "Reasoning Collapse" Research with 40% Brittleness Reduction

**Source & Date:** Skycrumbs Blog / DeepMind — August 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** DeepMind published research identifying "reasoning collapse" — the phenomenon where models fail on logically equivalent problems stated in different ways — and proposed a training methodology that reduces this brittleness by approximately 40%. Brittle reasoning is one of the clearest indicators that current LLMs lack genuine understanding. This work directly attacks a core weakness separating today's models from AGI-level robustness, making it one of the most technically significant alignment-adjacent research findings of the month.

**Link:** https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026
