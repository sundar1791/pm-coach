### 1. OpenAI Releases GPT-5.6 in Three Flavors

**Source:** Axios / Engadget — July 9, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** GPT-5.6 is OpenAI's most capable public model to date, shipped in three variants — Sol (maximum capability), Luna (speed-optimized), and Terra (balanced).
