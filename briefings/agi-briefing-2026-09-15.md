### 6. Anthropic, OpenAI & DeepMind Quietly Form AI Industry Standards Body

**Source:** Washington Post | **Date:** September 14, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** Dario Amodei, Sam Altman, and Google DeepMind leadership have held closed working-group sessions since July aimed at creating a self-regulatory AI standards body — an explicit alternative to waiting on governments. Voluntary industry coordination on safety standards, if it holds, could be the fastest path to norms that govern AGI development. The fact that direct commercial competitors are cooperating on safety frameworks is historically unusual and significant.

### 7. 2026 International AI Safety Report: Alignment Is Not Keeping Pace

**Source:** International AI Safety Report (Yoshua Bengio, lead) / The Hill | **Date:** September 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** The most comprehensive multilateral AI risk assessment to date — drawing on 100+ AI experts from 30+ nations — concludes that alignment research has not kept pace with capability development, and the mechanisms to close that gap are not yet in place. This is the most authoritative warning yet about the widening safety-capability gap.

### 10. Large Reasoning Models (LRMs): Parallel Reasoning Architectures Emerge

**Source:** Shakudo Blog / arXiv / LLM Stats | **Date:** September 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** A new class of models — Large Reasoning Models — uses explicit intermediate reasoning tokens and, in Grok 4.3's case, a four-agent parallel reasoning architecture with a synthesis layer.
