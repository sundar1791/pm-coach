### 5. DeepMind Paper Reduces Reasoning Collapse in AI Models by ~40%
**Source:** AI Research Highlights, Skycrumbs Blog | **Date:** August 2026

A DeepMind research paper tackled "reasoning collapse" — the phenomenon where AI models that correctly solve a problem fail when the same problem is restated differently. The paper proposes a novel training methodology that reduces this brittleness by approximately 40% on their benchmark. Eliminating reasoning collapse is critical for AGI, as true general intelligence must be robust to surface-level reformulations of identical underlying problems.

**Link:** https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026

### 7. EU AI Act Article 50 Transparency Rules Now Legally Enforceable
**Source:** Future of Life Institute AI Safety Index | **Date:** August 2, 2026

The EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations became enforceable on August 2, 2026, requiring providers to clearly label AI-generated content and disclose when users interact with AI systems. Penalties of up to 7% of global annual turnover apply for serious violations. This marks the beginning of a new regulatory era for AI deployment, with direct implications for how frontier labs can release and market AGI-adjacent systems in Europe.
