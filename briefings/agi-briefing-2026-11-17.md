### 10. RL-Trained 27B Reasoning Model Achieves 60% Success on Unseen Dual-Arm Robot

**Source:** samonai.substack.com | **Date:** Nov 13, 2026 (preprint)

**Why it matters for AGI:** A preprint describes a 27B-parameter reasoning model trained entirely via reinforcement learning in simulation — with no human demonstrations — that generalizes to an unseen dual-arm robot, succeeding in 18 of 30 trials. Zero-shot physical generalization from simulation to novel hardware is a long-standing hard problem; this result, if confirmed, suggests RL-based reasoning models may generalize across embodied systems without task-specific training data.

**Link:** https://samonai.substack.com/p/ai-brief-november-16-2026
