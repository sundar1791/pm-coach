### 3. GPT-6 Astra Launch & the Contested "AGI Era" Declaration

**Source:** Bloomberg, TradingKey | **Date:** September 3–6, 2026 (ongoing discussion)

**Why it matters for AGI:** OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra launch triggered the most prominent public claim yet that the AGI era has begun — with Nvidia's Jensen Huang declaring "AGI has arrived." OpenAI's president echoed the sentiment, though Sam Altman hedged with "not quite yet." The ARC Prize Foundation disputed the claim, saying OSWorld benchmark results don't represent "the complexity and open-endedness of the real world." This definitional battle is itself a milestone: the question is no longer hypothetical.

**Link:** [Bloomberg — What Is AGI?](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-09-04/what-is-agi-openai-anthropic-race-for-artificial-general-intelligence)

### 4. AI Agent Computer Task Accuracy: 12% → 66% in One Year (OSWorld)

**Source:** ARC Prize Foundation via web search | **Date:** 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** OSWorld measures whether AI agents can complete real computer tasks across operating systems — one of the most demanding proxies for general-purpose reasoning. A jump from ~12% to 66% accuracy in a single year is extraordinary. While the ARC Prize cautions the benchmark has limits, this trajectory, if sustained, points toward agents capable of meaningful autonomous knowledge work within 2–3 years.

**Link:** [AI Rankings — What is AGI?](https://theairankings.com/guides/what-is-agi/)

### 7. Paris Call on AI Safety — 6 Governments, 10 AI Companies Sign Declaration

**Source:** Government of Canada, CASRAI | **Date:** November 9, 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** The Paris Call brings together Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, Mistral AI, and Hugging Face under a government-backed safety framework — the first multi-jurisdictional AI safety declaration with both government and lab signatories. As systems approach AGI-level capabilities, coordinated safety governance becomes critical. The declaration requires independent third-party evaluation, which could become the template for AGI oversight.

**Link:** [Joint Declaration — Paris Call](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/statements/2026/11/09/joint-declaration-paris-call-ai-safety-and-security-explained)
