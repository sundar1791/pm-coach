### 5. "Selective Activation Sparsity" Training Method Matches Models 3× Larger
**Source:** ICML 2026 | **Date:** August 2026  
**Why it matters for AGI:** A paper at ICML 2026 introduced "selective activation sparsity," a training technique that teaches models to activate only the most relevant parameters per task. Models trained this way match the performance of models three times their size. This efficiency gain is significant for AGI timelines: more capable systems can now be built with dramatically less compute, lowering barriers and accelerating iteration cycles.
