## 8. Yann LeCun Publishes Proof That LeJEPA Can Recover World State

**Source:** ArXiv / AI Research | **Date:** June 2026

**Why it matters for AGI:** LeCun's new paper "When Does LeJEPA Learn a World Model?" formally proves that under Gaussian latent dynamics, LeJEPA can recover the hidden state behind nonlinear observations up to rotation. This is significant because LeCun has consistently argued that LLMs cannot achieve AGI, and this paper advances the non-autoregressive, world-model-centric alternative architecture that he believes is the actual path forward.

**Why it matters for AGI:** A key 2026 alignment breakthrough involves "meta-feedback" — having human evaluators critique a model's reasoning process rather than just its outputs.
