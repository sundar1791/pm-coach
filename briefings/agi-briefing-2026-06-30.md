# Daily AGI Briefing — 2026-06-30

**Executive Summary:** Compute, not algorithms, is the binding constraint on the road to AGI right now — Google is rationing its own Gemini models to Meta and paying SpaceX $920M/month for bridge GPU capacity, while Alphabet just closed the largest equity raise in U.S. corporate history to fund the buildout, even as frontier labs keep gating their most capable models behind government vetting rather than public release.

---

### 1. Google Rations Meta's Access to Gemini as AI Compute Demand Outstrips Supply
**Source & Date:** Financial Times, CNBC, Dataconomy — June 29, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Google's cloud division told Meta around March that it could not fulfill Gemini capacity requests, delaying internal Meta projects and pushing Meta to shift workloads to its own Muse Spark model. When the infrastructure leader with the most compute can't serve its own largest customers, it signals that compute scarcity — not model architecture — is now the rate-limiting step for frontier-scale progress industry-wide.
**Link:** https://www.cryptopolitan.com/google-meta-gemini-ai-demand-exceeds-supply/

### 2. Google to Pay SpaceX $920M/Month for 110,000 Nvidia GPUs Through 2029
**Source & Date:** CNBC, TechCrunch, SEC filing — June 5, 2026 (ongoing through June)
**Why it matters for AGI:** Google is leasing "bridge capacity" from SpaceX's (formerly xAI's) Colossus data centers in a deal worth roughly $30B over its lifetime, explicitly because demand for its Gemini Enterprise agent platform is outpacing its own data-center buildout. Frontier labs are now striking unconventional cross-company compute deals just to keep training and serving capacity ahead of agentic demand.
**Link:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/google-to-pay-spacex-920-million-a-month-for-xai-compute-capacity.html

### 3. Alphabet Closes Record $84.75B Equity Raise, Including $10B From Berkshire Hathaway
**Source & Date:** Bloomberg, CNBC, SEC filing — June 1–3, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** The raise — the largest equity capital raise in U.S. corporate history — funds AI infrastructure spend directly, and Berkshire Hathaway's $10B private placement is a notable signal of value-investor conviction that the AI buildout is durable, not speculative. Capital at this scale removes funding as a constraint on multi-year frontier training runs.
**Link:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/alphabet-to-raise-80-billion-in-equity-capital-for-ai-spending

### 4. Anthropic's Mythos 5 Partially Unblocked for Critical-Infrastructure Defenders; Fable 5 Still Suspended
**Source & Date:** CNN Business, CNBC, Fortune — June 26–27, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** The U.S. government revised its export block to let Mythos 5 — Anthropic's strongest cybersecurity-capable model — reach a small group of vetted cyber-defense organizations, while Fable 5 remains fully suspended for general users. This is the clearest case yet of a national government, not a lab, deciding the release schedule for a model class deemed AGI-adjacent on offensive-cyber capability.
**Link:** https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/26/tech/anthropic-mythos-release

### 5. OpenAI Splits GPT-5.6 Into Three Government-Vetted Tiers: Sol, Terra, Luna
**Source & Date:** OpenAI, Axios, Thurrott — June 26, 2026
**Why it matters for AGI:** Sol (flagship, hardest problems), Terra (balanced, 2x cheaper than GPT-5.5), and Luna (low-cost volume) all ship with strengthened safeguards for cyber and bio misuse, but are limited to ~20 companies whose access was individually approved by the government. Tiered capability plus tiered government access is becoming the standard release pattern for frontier-class models, not the exception.
**Link:** https://www.axios.com/2026/06/26/openai-gpt-sol-terra-luna-trump

### 6. Anthropic Data: Longest Reliable Agent Autonomy Nearly Doubled in 3 Months
